10 Anti-patterns
Dark-mode editor as the default still.
Naymee coral #F74B4A as Opacity’s accent.
Figma purple or Jeff #7D20FF as the selection stroke.
White check on teal (loses the inverted disc).
Send as a rounded-rect, or Approve as a circle.
Twelve inspector sections. This still is three.
Glassmorphism over the canvas.
Punched-square mark in the top-left (that slot is the project).
Relabel Fill/Hug to width 100% / height auto.
Sparkle-first toolbar.
