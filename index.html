09 Copy / Voice
Keep

“For thirty years, design and code have been separate worlds.” “No more separation. No more translation. Design IS code.” “You’re a visual builder, not a vibe coder.” “Join the waitlist.”

Verbs

Design, version, publish, install, branch, merge, deploy.

Do not

Lowercase-startup whisper. “Reimagine.” Fake metrics. “Figma killer.”

10 Anti-patterns
Fill the hole, round the mark into an iOS icon, or use the overlapping-circle O.
Dark “devtool” marketing page.
Promote unused accents #6B5CFF / #FF8A4C as the brand.
Geist / Satoshi / another Inter cousin for headlines.
Anonymous colored dots instead of named cursors.
Rainbow mesh behind every section.
Card grids, testimonials, metric counters, process timelines.
Treat Naymee’s coral file-icon as Opacity’s mark.
Ease-in on small chrome. depth is for the logo whip only.
Animate keyboard-driven actions.
If not, delete the extra font and punch the hole again.
