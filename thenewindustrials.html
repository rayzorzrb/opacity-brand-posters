10 Field notes
Category

Editorial / cultural. Named work with Auger, Anduril, Saronic, Seasats, Epirus. Stay named.

Keep

“IRL.” “An Unusual Design Company.” “They’re not waiting to get established to worry about Design.”

Verbs

Rebuild, reshape, craft, take control, make things differently. CTA: then we’d love to chat.

Do not

“Revolutionize.” “Disrupting legacy.” “Hard tech thesis.” “The future of X.”

Case films

Anduril cyan is Anduril’s. Do not grade case films to orange. Do not replace them with stock.

Anti

Dark defense-tech sites with cyan HUD chrome. That’s Anduril’s product, not this issue.

Anti

Card grids, testimonials, metric counters, process timelines. This is a scroll essay.

Anti

Glassmorphism, fat shadows, SaaS pills, crypto glow. No cast shadows on the paper.

Anti

Orange as a page background after the preloader. It had its one moment.

Anti
