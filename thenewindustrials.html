07 Imagery

Family A — hexagonal plates, gradient pillars, cream void, almost no shadow. Family B — real vessels, tablets, dusk. Anduril cyan is Anduril’s, not this issue’s. Do not grade case films to orange.

09 Copy / Voice
Keep

“IRL.” “BIG big. Fusion reactor big.” “The opposite of by Design is by accident.” “An Unusual Design Company.” “They’re not waiting to get established to worry about Design.”

Verbs

Rebuild, reshape, craft, take control, make things differently.

Do not

“Revolutionize.” “Disrupting legacy.” “Hard tech thesis.” “The future of X.” Stay named: Auger, Anduril, Saronic, Seasats, Epirus.

10 Anti-patterns

Raw Materials and TNI on equal header weight.
